About

Research with a systems perspective.

I am a Computer Science researcher with a background in concurrent algorithms, synchronization primitives, formal verification, and high-performance computing. My doctoral research focused on scalable synchronization mechanisms for modern multicore systems and on improving their robustness against programming errors.

My current interests extend toward compiler infrastructure, particularly LLVM and MLIR, with the goal of connecting compiler techniques with efficient and reliable parallel systems.
