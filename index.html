About

Welcome to my Page !!.

I am a PhD scholar in the Dept. of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Dharwad working with Prof. Nikhil Hegde. I am associated with the Scalability, Programming Environment and Compilers (SPEC) lab. My research interests lie at the intersection of concurrent systems, formal verification, and multicore scalability. My PhD research focuses on resilient synchronization lock primitives -- designing synchronization mechanisms that remain correct under misus in modern multi-core architectures. I also work with model checking and formal methods, including SPIN/Promela.

I completed M.Tech and B.Tech from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur and Government College of Engineering, Amravati, Maharashtra respectively. Prior to joining IIT Dharwad, I was working with Academic Institutions--UPES Dehradun, Nirma University, Parul University.

More recently, I have been expanding toward compiler infrastructure, particularly LLVM and MLIR, for high-performance and parallel applications.

Research

Research directions

01

Concurrent Systems

Scalable synchronization algorithms and concurrent data structures for modern multicore and NUMA systems.

02

Lock Shielding

Misuse-resilient synchronization that detects and handles unbalanced lock and unlock operations.

03

Formal Verification

Safety, liveness, fairness, and parameterized reasoning for synchronization algorithms using model checking.

04

High-Performance Computing

Performance analysis using multicore benchmarks, CPU affinity, hardware counters, and NUMA-aware experimentation.

05

Compiler Technologies

Exploring LLVM and MLIR for compiler-based optimization of parallel and high-performance software.

06

Computer Architecture

Understanding the interaction between synchronization, memory systems, processor architecture, and scalability.

Projects

Research software & projects

01

Lock Shielding

A framework for making synchronization primitives resilient to programming errors. Implementations explore thread-local lock tracking, array-based tracking, hash-assisted tracking, reentrancy detection, and unbalanced lock/unlock detection.

C/C++ · POSIX threads · TLS · multicore systems

02

Formal Verification of Synchronization Algorithms

Verification models for scalable locks using explicit-state and symbolic model checking, with safety, liveness, fairness, and parameterized verification considerations.

SPIN · Promela · NuSMV · LTL · counter abstraction

03

Lock Performance Analysis

Experimental evaluation of synchronization algorithms using hardware performance counters and parallel benchmark suites.

Linux · perf · PAPI · PARSEC · SPLASH-2x · Synchrobench

04

Compiler & MLIR Research

Exploring LLVM and MLIR as platforms for compiler research, intermediate representations, and architecture-aware optimization of parallel applications.

LLVM · MLIR · SSA · compiler optimization
